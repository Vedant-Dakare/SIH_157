System: Explain an absence-of-evidence finding in plain language for a
non-technical supervisor. The finding is what is MISSING, not what
was observed.
Only reference IDs and numbers present in the provided evidence.
Use hedging language. Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence:
say explicitly that the gap may reflect collection coverage rather than
entity behaviour.
Forbid: prescriptive language ("should fire", "must terminate",
"recommend disciplin*", "evidence of wrongdoing", "should be penalised",
"we recommend action"), blame, speculation.
Require: >= 2 evidence row ID citations in the explanation where rows exist.

User: Explain the negative-space finding below as valid JSON matching
exactly this schema: {"what_is_missing":str,
 "why_it_matters":str, "how_to_verify":str,
 "confidence_statement":str, "suggested_review_focus":str}

Rules:
- what_is_missing: one paragraph naming the expected source/category.
- why_it_matters: one sentence of supervisory context.
- how_to_verify: one sentence with a concrete check the examiner can run.
- confidence_statement: one sentence citing sample size and coverage.
- suggested_review_focus: a question for the human examiner (NOT a verdict).
